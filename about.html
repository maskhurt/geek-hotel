About The Geed Hotel

A 3-star hotel built around one idea — comfortable, honest hospitality in the middle of the city.

Who We Are

Affordable luxury, right on Moi Avenue

The Geed Hotel sits on Moi Avenue at Biashara Plaza, in the middle of Nairobi's Central Business District. We built the hotel for people who need to be close to the action — corporate travellers with early meetings, tourists exploring the city on foot, and couples wanting a comfortable, central base — without paying CBD prices for it.

Every room is kept clean and comfortable through daily housekeeping, and our reception team is on duty 24 hours a day so you're never without help, whatever time you arrive or check out. Guests consistently tell us the same things: the location is unbeatable, the staff are friendly and attentive, and the rooms are exactly what they need for a good night's rest between a busy day of meetings, shopping or sightseeing.

As a 3-star hotel, we focus on doing the essentials well — clean rooms, reliable Wi-Fi, hot water, a welcoming restaurant and a team that treats every guest, whether staying one night or several, with the same level of care.
